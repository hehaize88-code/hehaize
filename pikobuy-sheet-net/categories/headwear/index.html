Headwear: 2 product finds

This category page groups verified main-site paths and adds checks specific to headwear. Hats and goggles depend on shape, adjustment range and small visible details.

What to check in warehouse photos

  • Confirm the exact model, colour and included parts.
  • Check crown or frame symmetry, embroidery and straps.
  • Inspect fasteners, lens or brim condition and labels.

Size and option note

Check circumference, strap range or frame measurements instead of assuming one size fits all.

Weight and parcel note

Rigid brims, frames and lenses may need protection that adds parcel volume.
