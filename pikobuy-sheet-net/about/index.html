About the project

About PikoBuy Sheet and Our Research Method

PikoBuy Sheet is an independent research website that organises product discovery and explains ordering, warehouse review and parcel planning. Learn how listings, policy claims and update dates are checked.

What we publish

We publish organised product paths, category indexes, QC checklists, shipping explainers and beginner guides. Product links open the full external catalogue so readers can inspect live details.

How facts are checked

Platform claims are checked against PikoBuy’s official About, Beginner’s Guide, Shipping Cost Estimation, Shipping Policy and Returns & Exchanges pages. Changeable fees are never presented as permanent.

What a listing does not prove

Inclusion does not guarantee authenticity, stock, seller performance, materials, durability or fit. Warehouse photos help with visible inspection but cannot prove every quality claim.
