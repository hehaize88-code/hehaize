What to check in warehouse photos

  • Confirm both shoes, colour and selected size.
  • Compare toe shape, heel height, panels and pair symmetry.
  • Inspect outsole, glue, labels and visible damage.

Size and option note

Use the seller chart and warehouse label; compare insole length when fit is uncertain.

Weight and parcel note

Shoeboxes can add volume. Compare protection retained with box removal only when the item can tolerate it.
