## Verified fact dossier

The official guide supports a two-stage workflow: select and pay for an item; later choose warehoused goods and arrange international delivery. It names Taobao, Tmall, 1688 and Weidian links. Warehouse photos appear after arrival, and parcel tracking is under Parcels. This is an agent workflow, not merely a directory of spreadsheets.

The inspection answer narrows the broad homepage marketing: visible color, requested size and significant damage are inspected; fine differences are not fully guaranteed. Electronics are inspected externally with accessories, without powering on to test functionality. Returns depend on seller agreement, condition and order status. Removed tags or packaging can affect eligibility. The published return request window is five days after stock-in, but public evergreen copy should direct buyers to the current order's aftersales deadline rather than promise universal eligibility.

The estimator is useful for collecting parcel inputs, not for quoting a universal rate. Route eligibility can change with parcel size, weight and goods. The fee article separates domestic delivery from international carriage; it does not justify a claim that a displayed catalog price includes everything.

## Conflicts and claims to exclude

- Estimator inconsistency: the visual example divides volume by 8000, while explanatory prose uses 6000. Do not publish a universal divisor. Explain that the selected route's charging rule governs.
- Homepage free-return wording is broader than the help center's eligibility and cost conditions. Do not promise unconditional free returns.
- Official promotion displays differ even between indexed homepage variants. Do not publish a fixed coupon value, permanently available coupon, guaranteed discount or registration reward.
- No reliable official evidence found for a universal free-storage duration, a universal number of free QC photos, a complete current payment-method list, fixed refund settlement timing, or country-by-country delivery guarantees. Do not fill these gaps with competitor claims.
- The fee article's customs-declaration handling reference must not be presented as a destination's total import tax. No country-specific tax thresholds were researched for this task.
- Official image-search availability is not proof that CNBuyCha exposes an image-search endpoint. The preview must label only verified implemented capabilities.
- A seller photograph, public QC image and the buyer's own warehouse QC image are different things. Do not label all imported catalog images QC.
- An official product link does not establish authenticity, stock, an endorsement or permission to sell a particular item. Avoid fabricated "verified", "trusted seller" or "tested by us" badges.
