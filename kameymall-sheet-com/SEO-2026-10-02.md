# KameyMall Sheet — manual SEO/content release, 2026-10-02

## Scope and automation

This release changes only `kameymall-sheet-com/` in the shared repository. No other site's source, deployment configuration or automation has been edited.

The existing multi-site automation already permanently excludes this domain and repository path, effective 2026-09-10. That task and the separate KameyMall SEO follow-up were already disabled when inspected. Their enablement, schedules, cursors and other-site instructions are preserved. This is a user-requested manual release; it does not resume automated article publication.

## Evidence and limits

- GSC property: `sc-domain:kameymall-sheet.com`.
- Settled window 2026-09-02 through 2026-09-29: 0 clicks, 43 impressions, average position 15.26. Previous 28 days: 0 clicks, 104 impressions, average position 22.39. The sample is too small to attribute zero clicks to a specific title or predict a traffic uplift.
- Visible 90-day queries are mostly sparse brand variations. GSC anonymization means missing query rows cannot establish zero search demand.
- Homepage and spreadsheet guide passed URL inspection. The USA shipping guide was discovered but not indexed at inspection time.
- Previously published sitemap contained 370 URLs with no reported errors or warnings. The API's legacy indexed-count field is not evidence that the entire site is unindexed.
- GA4 reporting access was unavailable because the connected Google account lacked Analytics scope; Bing reporting was not configured. No GA4 sessions, Bing volumes or competitor traffic estimates were fabricated.
- Search and product destinations were checked independently. Keep `/AllProducts/?q=...`; the historical `/search.html?keywords=...` route returned 404.

## Content decisions

Public competitor review covered kameymallspreadsheets.com, kameymallspreadsheet.org and kameymallindex.com/en. Their category-led discovery, concrete product references and sizing/QC context informed the four new topics. These are intent-based opportunities, not keywords proven to have a particular monthly volume.

| New URL suffix | Primary keyword | Supporting intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kameymall-hoodie-finds | kameymall hoodie finds | sweatshirt finds, hoodie sizing, garment measurements |
| kameymall-football-jersey-finds | kameymall football jersey | jersey finds, fan/player options, print checks |
| kameymall-size-guide | kameymall size guide | size chart, clothing measurements, shoe sizing |
| kameymall-budget-finds-under-100-cny | kameymall budget finds | finds under 100 CNY, item price versus delivered budget |

All four articles contain original English editorial content and German, French, Spanish, Italian and Polish versions. The ten previously English-only articles now have complete translated bodies and reciprocal language alternatives. Thirteen existing English articles have refreshed navigation; the spreadsheet, shipping-cost and QC guides also receive substantive practical additions. Translations were machine-assisted and checked for structural completeness, retained numerical examples, dates and links; no native-speaker certification is claimed.

The catalog's September 1 price snapshots remain explicitly dated. New illustrative calculations are labeled as examples, not live quotes. Existing official-policy review dates are retained. Article images reuse catalog illustrations and are explicitly distinguished from actual warehouse QC photos. The Nike sweatshirt and Premier League jersey destinations were reachable on October 2.

## Technical changes

- 24 articles per language; 444 canonical sitemap URLs in total.
- Localized article cards and related links now stay in the selected language.
- New articles lead the article grid, with exact product-reference links and category-specific guidance.
- Visible article images also appear in Article/Open Graph metadata; publisher logo and favicon metadata are explicit.
- Source and Cloudflare Pages generated bundle must be deployed together using `npm run build:pages`.
- Existing product counts, outbound targets and GA4 event implementation are preserved.
- Page content is supplied from the server for the selected language and article. The browser's site-page JavaScript chunk drops from about 252 kB gzip to about 22 kB gzip; this is a bundle-size comparison, not a measured Core Web Vitals improvement.
