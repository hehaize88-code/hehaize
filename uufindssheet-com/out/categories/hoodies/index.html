Short filtering guide

Narrow the category without guessing.

Use product data to shortlist first, then use matched QC photos to inspect the exact listing rather than a look-alike.

FILTER 01

Pick a construction

Decide between a hoodie, crewneck sweatshirt or lighter knit before comparing graphics and price.

Keep the listing ID with your notes.

FILTER 02

Compare measurements

Check chest width, body length, sleeve length and shoulder line rather than relying only on a letter size.

Keep the listing ID with your notes.

FILTER 03

Inspect placement

For printed or embroidered pieces, compare scale, centring, edge finish and distance from seams.

Keep the listing ID with your notes.

FILTER 04

Check the live variant

Multi-style listings need an extra check that the selected colour or design matches the QC set you reviewed.

Keep the listing ID with your notes.
