UUFINDS SPREADSHEET GUIDE

A useful UUFinds spreadsheet starts with the exact product.

A spreadsheet brings product names, pictures, prices and source links together. Use it to build a shortlist, then compare the exact item and available photos before following a purchase link. A similar image alone does not identify the same seller or product.

01

Search and sort your finds

Search by a product name or source product ID. Filter by category, then sort by latest additions, popularity or price. Image search compares your uploaded photo with catalogue images to surface visually similar candidates.

02

Read product photos and QC carefully

Open the product gallery and compare multiple views. Look at shape, stitching, labels and measurements where visible. Keep the product ID and selected option in view: catalogue pictures can include seller images and reference QC, and are not a guarantee of the item you will receive.

03

Continue on UUFinds

Each product button passes that product’s own source ID to UUFinds. Re-check the listing, available options and current price there. The registration button opens the official UUFinds registration page.
