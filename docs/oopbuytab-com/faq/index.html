A few things worth knowing.

Straight answers about the spreadsheet, product photos and buying links.

Is OOPBuy Tab the official OOPBuy website?+

OOPBuy Tab is an independent product discovery site. Registration, orders and payments take place on oopbuy.com. Purchase links include an affiliate invite code; no discount is promised.

Where do the products and prices come from?+

Product listings, images, search results and sorting come from CNBuyCha. Prices are displayed in USD across all languages. Confirm the current price, availability and selected variant in OOPBuy.

Are the product photos warehouse QC photos?+

The displayed images come from CNBuyCha. Their origin as warehouse QC is not verified. Check the photos of your own order in OOPBuy before deciding to ship.

Does the item price include delivery?+

Treat the displayed amount as an estimated item price. Review domestic postage, international delivery, optional services and any applicable charges in the current checkout and parcel quote.

How do the purchase links work?+

The button on a product page opens its original Weidian item ID in OOPBuy. The CNBuyCha catalogue number is a separate ID. Check the destination listing before you order.

Can I search with an image?+

Image search is not available in this preview. You can search by name or browse the categories. We do not substitute unrelated results for an uploaded photo.
