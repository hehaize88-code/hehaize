How to use OOPBuy

Follow the buying process from a useful product link to an informed warehouse check and a considered parcel decision.

01

Start with a clear shortlist

+

Choose a product category, then compare a few relevant listings. Record the product name, source link, selected colour, size and quantity. The directory price is a useful starting reference; check the current option and amount when you open the product through OOPBuy. Keep questions about measurements, materials or included accessories with the item. A small shortlist makes it easier to notice when two similar pictures actually represent different versions or different seller options.

02

Review the order before paying

+

OOPBuy’s beginner guide describes purchasing through the platform and receiving goods at its warehouse in China. The product and Chinese domestic delivery are paid first; international parcel shipping is paid separately later. Before ordering, inspect the loaded listing and match its options to your notes. Review the amount and the conditions shown for that item. Resolve an unclear size, quantity or product restriction before payment. Save the order reference so the later warehouse information can be matched to the right purchase.

03

Check the item when warehouse information appears

+

Seller dispatch and warehouse processing are separate stages. Once your order has received warehouse information, read its current status and inspect the available photos. Compare the received colour, size label, quantity and visible condition with the option you ordered. If a useful detail cannot be seen, ask whether an additional photo or measurement is available and what it costs. Check the current aftersales options promptly when there is a problem, because eligibility depends on the item and its circumstances.

04

Make the parcel decision last

+

Before submitting a parcel, review every included item, the delivery address, the selected route and its conditions. Use the official shipping estimate to explore suitable options, then check the information shown for your actual parcel. Packaging, measured dimensions and the contents can affect the available choices. Keep the order and parcel references together, and follow the official account for support and tracking. Our detailed guides explain the sizing, QC, cost and packing decisions you may need along this sequence.
