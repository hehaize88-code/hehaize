A Hipobuy product link should open the exact seller item you intend to buy. Start with the original Taobao or Weidian listing, keep its marketplace and item ID together, then check the image, variant and current price on Hipobuy. A catalogue record number, an agent order number and a seller item ID are different identifiers.

[Browse the product catalogue](/en/catalog/) or read [Custom Order versus Forwarding](/en/articles/hipobuy-custom-order-forwarding/) if the ordinary link search cannot complete your purchase request.

## What a product link actually identifies

A marketplace listing describes an offer from a seller. It may contain several sizes, colours or versions, each with a different price or availability. The link normally identifies the listing rather than a permanently reserved combination of options. Saving a URL is therefore only the first part of saving your choice. Keep the selected option, seller name and date with it. A picture that looks correct does not establish that the same size is still available when you return.

The catalogue on this site has its own record numbers and product pages. Those pages help you discover products and inspect the images supplied by the source catalogue. They do not create a purchase, reserve stock or confirm the authenticity of an item. Once you move to Hipobuy, read the destination page and select the options again if needed. The active purchase form determines what you are requesting, not the older directory card.

## Keep the marketplace with the item ID

A number without its platform is incomplete. A Weidian item ID belongs to a Weidian listing; a Taobao item ID belongs to a Taobao listing. Do not place a Taobao number into a Weidian purchase path merely because both contain digits. A source link may also include sharing or referral parameters. Keep enough of the original URL to recover the item, and check the destination before simplifying anything you do not understand.

| Record | What it identifies | Where it belongs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller item ID | An offer on its original marketplace | Product lookup with the correct marketplace |
| Catalogue record | A page in this independent directory | Finding the saved catalogue page |
| Hipobuy order ID | Your purchase request | Order history and support |
| Parcel tracking number | A logistics shipment | The corresponding carrier or parcel record |

For example, catalogue record 3458 is associated with original Weidian item ID 7867456361 in this site's verified link mapping. The Hipobuy button uses the original seller ID, not 3458. This example explains the mapping; it is not a recommendation to purchase that item or a statement that its current options are unchanged.

## Open a Weidian find from this catalogue

Open the product card, inspect its title and gallery, and note the displayed product ID. Use the “Open in Hipobuy” button to reach the mapped original Weidian item. Compare the destination image and option list with your saved choice. A title may be translated differently while the underlying product is the same, so use several matching details rather than the title alone. Stop if the model, seller or available versions no longer make sense.

Our purchase links use Hipobuy's Weidian product path with the original item ID. That convenience is not a universal converter for every marketplace or every agent's internal link. If you arrived from a different spreadsheet, first recover its original seller listing. Copying the final digits from an unrelated agent URL can carry an internal record number instead of the source ID. Repeatedly trying guessed numbers is less useful than locating the seller URL once.

## Use a Taobao link without guessing a conversion

For a Taobao item, start with its original product page or the seller's share link and use the link search on the official Hipobuy homepage. Verify what the search resolves before ordering. This directory's current mapped purchase buttons are for Weidian items; they do not establish a supported Taobao URL pattern. Do not change the platform segment of a URL and assume the result is equivalent. Support can change, and an individual listing may require more information.

If the ordinary search cannot load the offer, check the original listing in its own marketplace. A deleted offer, unavailable option or restricted item will not be repaired merely by changing the address format. When the seller page remains valid, the official Custom Order form offers a way to provide a product link, title, specifications and purchasing notes for review. Confirm acceptance and the actual price before treating the request as ready to buy.

## A link that fails needs a diagnosis

Separate an invalid source from a temporary loading problem. Check whether the original URL still opens, whether it points to a product rather than a shop homepage, and whether a short sharing link resolves to the intended item. Then compare the marketplace and ID with the Hipobuy destination. If a page asks you to sign in, use the official site directly. Do not enter account credentials into an independent converter or a page pretending to be the platform login.

If the item loads but has no usable options, the issue may be availability rather than URL formatting. If the page shows a different product, preserve the original evidence and stop the purchase attempt. If the seller details are missing, ask for clarification instead of substituting a visually similar listing. A replacement may have a different seller, material, size chart and return arrangement even when its main photograph looks identical.

## Verify the variant and the real payable amount

Write down the exact colour, size, quantity and version before adding the item to a purchase request. Check whether the displayed price applies to those selections and whether domestic shipping is separate. Catalogue USD prices are discovery references, not delivered totals. Hipobuy's Custom Order form separates item price and domestic delivery, and explicitly excludes international shipping from its initial total. Keep the currencies clear when comparing those amounts with a directory price.

A useful saved record contains the source URL, selected variant, current quoted amount and the question still unresolved. Avoid copying a size chart from another seller or assuming that a word such as “premium” describes a tested property. If a seller changes the offer between discovery and payment, decide using the current details. The point of retaining the link is to make that change visible, not to make the original snapshot binding.

## Treat catalogue pictures and QC as separate evidence

The gallery helps identify the product and compare visible features. It does not by itself prove that the pictures show the unit purchased for your order. Your warehouse QC set should be connected to the actual order and selected variant. Compare labels, construction, included parts and useful measurements after the item arrives. Ask for a particular missing angle when the decision depends on something the existing photos cannot show.

Read the [QC photo checklist](/en/articles/qc-photo-checklist/) before approving international packing. Photos cannot establish every claim about materials, durability or authenticity. Keep any relevant tags and packaging while a return decision is unresolved, and check the current return conditions promptly. Finding a working link is the start of a purchase process; it is not a substitute for reviewing the received item.

## Keep a simple record through the handoffs

Use one note per intended item. Begin with the original seller link and option, add the Hipobuy order ID when a purchase request exists, and later add the parcel record containing that order. Keep domestic tracking separate from international tracking. This makes it easier to explain a problem without sending support several unrelated numbers or expecting a carrier to locate a product listing.

Before continuing, confirm three things: the destination is the official Hipobuy site, the original item and selected option match, and the unresolved cost or delivery questions are recorded. Use [the shipping calculator guide](/en/articles/hipobuy-shipping-calculator/) to plan packed weight separately. This workflow helps you preserve product identity from discovery through delivery without assuming that a link conversion verifies the seller or guarantees an outcome.
