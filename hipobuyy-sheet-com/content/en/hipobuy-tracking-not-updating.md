## Start with the last confirmed event

When Hipobuy tracking is not updating, the useful starting point is the last event you can actually verify. A quiet tracking page does not, by itself, show whether a parcel is waiting for collection, travelling between scanning points, undergoing a handover or facing a delivery problem. Record the exact wording, event time and source before deciding what to do. The purpose of this guide is to turn an unclear status into a specific check, not to predict a delivery date without information about the route.

Keep three records separate: the product order, the international parcel record and the carrier tracking record. They describe different parts of the journey. A seller can dispatch an item domestically before it reaches the warehouse; the warehouse can receive that item before you submit an international parcel. Searching an internal order number on a carrier website will not necessarily produce a result. First confirm which identifier belongs to the shipment you are trying to follow.

## Distinguish seller delivery from international shipping

Open the individual item order and identify whether the seller has sent it to the warehouse. If the item is still being purchased or awaiting seller dispatch, there is no reason to expect international carrier scans. If domestic tracking shows delivery to a warehouse, that establishes a domestic transport event. It does not establish that inspection, storage, parcel selection and international payment have all finished. Check those stages in their own records.

Hipobuy's published order-status guidance distinguishes domestic shipment, warehouse receipt, storage and international parcel handling. Status labels may change with the interface, so read the explanation attached to the current order rather than translating one English word in isolation. Save any request for additional payment, confirmation or product information. An unresolved account action can explain why the next stage has not started, even though an earlier seller shipment appears complete.

## Read a tracking gap by stage

| Last evidence | Check next | Avoid assuming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller dispatched the item | Domestic shipment and warehouse receipt | International shipping has started |
| Warehouse received the item | Inspection, selected items and parcel submission | The carrier has collected a parcel |
| Parcel awaiting packing or confirmation | Packing progress and any outstanding action | A carrier tracking failure |
| Tracking number created | Collection or acceptance event for that number | Physical possession by the carrier |
| Export or transfer event | The next named carrier and any linked number | A guaranteed next scan date |
| Delivery exception or collection notice | The stated action and deadline | Waiting alone will resolve it |

This table is a reading method, not a list of guaranteed Hipobuy interface labels. Use the wording in your own parcel and carrier records. Some services describe a handover differently, and a translated summary can omit useful details. Where the two sources disagree, keep both timestamps and ask which record is current. Do not erase the older evidence merely because a newer summary sounds more reassuring.

## Check the number and the carrier together

Copy the tracking number from the parcel record rather than retyping it from a screenshot. Look for spaces, similar-looking letters or digits and an accidentally copied order identifier. Confirm that the carrier you are checking is the one named for that parcel or for the relevant delivery stage. A service name used at checkout and the company performing local delivery may be different; a second identifier may appear when the parcel changes hands.

An aggregator can be a convenient overview, but it is not proof that every event has been imported correctly. Compare important exception messages with the service's own parcel information and the named carrier's current record. If no number has been supplied, ask for the applicable tracking identifier and collection status. Repeatedly trying unrelated numbers does not help establish whether the parcel has left the warehouse.

## Build a short evidence timeline

Create one row for each confirmed change, using the event timestamp rather than only the day you noticed it. Include the source, exact status, location if shown and any action you took. Keep the timezone where it is available. Two events displayed on different local dates can still describe the same handover. A translation such as “sent” is less useful than the original wording plus the system that displayed it.

For example, an illustrative timeline might show a parcel created on Monday, a number issued on Tuesday and carrier acceptance on Thursday. Those are three different events; the period before Thursday should not automatically be counted as time in the carrier's network. This example contains no promised Hipobuy processing times. Its value is showing why you should measure a delay from the relevant milestone instead of from the moment you placed the product order.

## Match your next action to the evidence

A record that says information was received but does not show collection calls for a collection-status check. A parcel with a recent carrier acceptance event calls for comparison against that route's stated service information. An explicit address problem, request for documents or collection deadline calls for the stated action through the service responsible. Read the full exception message before selecting a response; several unrelated situations can appear as a short “pending” label.

Do not use a fixed number of silent days as a universal lost-parcel rule. Routes, weekends, handovers and the kind of tracking service differ. Compare the silence with the current information for the route you paid for and any action deadline shown in the record. If that information is missing or contradictory, ask for clarification. A specific request is more useful than guessing a delivery date from a different customer's destination and parcel.

## Ask support one answerable question

Prepare the parcel identifier, route name, destination country, last confirmed event and timestamp, and screenshots of any conflicting records. Explain the narrow uncertainty: whether collection occurred, which carrier has the parcel, whether a new tracking number exists or whether you must complete an action. You can also ask when another check would be appropriate according to the selected service. This gives support something concrete to investigate.

Keep personal addresses, telephone numbers and complete labels out of public comments or screenshots. Provide necessary account information only through the service handling your order. Retain the reply alongside your timeline, including any promised follow-up date. Multiple vague messages in different channels can make it harder to see which question was answered. If you follow up, refer to the unresolved point and include any new event since the previous response.

## Handle delivery exceptions promptly

When tracking reports attempted delivery, collection availability or an address issue, inspect the current notice for the responsible delivery service and the required next step. Do not assume that the shopping agent can change every local-delivery instruction after dispatch. Use the options actually offered for that shipment, confirm any correction and retain the reference. An unrelated text message demanding payment is not sufficient evidence that it belongs to your parcel.

If a parcel is marked delivered but cannot be located, check the delivery details that are legitimately available for your shipment and ask the responsible service about the recorded handover. Keep your factual account precise. If it arrives damaged, document the outer packaging and contents before discarding evidence. Tracking is one part of a claim record, alongside item records and receipts; it does not by itself establish compensation eligibility or a guaranteed outcome.

## Close the record after the parcel arrives

Save the route, final delivery event, packed weight where known and the amount actually paid. This turns your own shipment into a useful reference for later planning. Keep the limitations attached: another season, destination or package size may produce a different experience. If you share an account publicly, distinguish what you observed from what support explained and from what remains unknown. Avoid turning one successful or delayed parcel into a universal platform claim.

For the next purchase, record the selected variant and domestic order before the warehouse stage, finish QC before parcel submission and save the final route quote. These small steps make later tracking questions easier to answer.
