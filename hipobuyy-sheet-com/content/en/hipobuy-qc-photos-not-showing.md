If Hipobuy QC photos are missing, first identify what you are looking at: a public product gallery, a seller shipment, or your own warehoused order. Public listing pictures do not establish that your item has been inspected. Check the order status and exact variant before requesting the missing evidence through your Hipobuy account.

## Find the right item before looking for photos

Keep the original product link, the selected size or colour and the order identifier together. A catalogue can contain several similar products, while one seller page can offer several different variants. An image search result is a visual match to investigate, not confirmation that it shows the same seller, batch or item that you ordered. A number used inside a directory may also differ from the original marketplace product identifier.

Open the corresponding order in your own account and compare its saved option with the purchase record. If you ordered two sizes of the same design, check each record separately. Seeing a photograph of one item does not resolve the other. Our [product-link guide](/en/articles/hipobuy-product-links/) explains how to preserve the original source when moving from a spreadsheet to a purchase. This directory cannot retrieve private photos from your account.

## Separate a listing gallery from warehouse evidence

A seller image illustrates an offer. A warehouse image should be associated with the item received for an order. A public gallery may contain useful views without establishing who took them or which purchase they document. Read the image context before using a photograph to approve colour, measurements or an important detail. A familiar backdrop alone does not prove that the photo belongs to your parcel.

The catalogue on this site provides source product pictures and links. It is useful for discovery, but those pictures are not represented as authenticated QC evidence for your own order. If you were searching for a Hipobuy QC finder, use the product identifier to locate the right listing first, then consult the order's warehouse record for your own inspection. Do not approve shipment just because a public photo looks convincing.

## Read the warehouse stage correctly

Hipobuy's official status guide describes Arrived as receipt at the warehouse while inspection is still pending. That means a domestic delivery scan and a complete inspection record are different milestones. Stored describes the storage stage. Neither a directory result nor a seller's dispatch message can establish that your inspection photographs are ready. Read the current explanation attached to your order if the interface wording differs.

| What you can see | What it establishes | Useful next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public product gallery | Pictures associated with a listing | Match the source and selected option |
| Seller dispatch or domestic delivery | A seller-to-warehouse transport event | Check the order's warehouse stage |
| Arrived | Receipt, with inspection pending in the official guide | Check inspection progress in the order |
| Stored but no usable pictures | Storage status, with evidence still missing for your decision | Ask where the inspection record is available |
| Pictures of the wrong option | A mismatch needing clarification | Provide the order and saved option |

## Check whether images are missing or failing to load

Before requesting a new inspection, distinguish an absent photo record from a display problem. Reopen the correct order and check whether the page shows an empty section, a loading message, a broken image or a permission prompt. These are different observations. If the same photo opens at full size but its thumbnail fails, report that behaviour instead of saying that no warehouse photos exist.

You can retry through your normal signed-in browser or the official interface you usually use. Avoid sharing a login, password or account access with a third-party photo finder. Keep any screenshot focused on the error and item reference; redact addresses and payment information before posting publicly. If the record remains unavailable, ask support whether the image was created and where it should be visible. Repeated refreshing cannot confirm warehouse progress.

## Request the view that changes your decision

An extra photograph is useful when it answers a specific unresolved question. For clothing, that might be the size label, chest width laid flat, sleeve length from a defined seam, or the back of a printed design. For footwear, you might need both shoes together, an outsole view or a readable size label. For an accessory, identify the closure, attachment or included component that needs to be visible.

Specify the order, variant, location on the item and intended measurement method. “Please show the label and measure the chest flat from edge to edge” gives a clearer task than “better QC.” Ask about current availability and any charge before authorising an additional service. Photographs can document visible details; they do not establish fibre composition, long-term durability or authenticity. The [QC checklist](/en/articles/qc-photo-checklist/) covers the inspection itself once the evidence is available.

## Keep the return decision separate from waiting for photos

If you already see a material mismatch, record it and contact Hipobuy promptly rather than waiting indefinitely for a perfect image set. The official returns page gives eligible requests a five-day, 120-hour window beginning the next hour after the status becomes Warehoused, with seller and condition requirements. The longer storage period is a different policy. Do not assume an unanswered photo request pauses a return deadline.

Check the exact time and applicable terms in your own order. Preserve tags, packaging and accessories while the decision is unresolved; asking for their removal can affect return eligibility. If you need clarification about the deadline or missing evidence, state both issues in the same support request. Our [warehouse return guide](/en/articles/return-policy-warehouse/) explains the policy conditions. It cannot decide an individual case or guarantee that a seller accepts a return.

## Use a compact evidence request

Prepare a short record with the order reference, selected variant, current status and timestamp, the missing image or measurement, and the decision you cannot yet make. Add a cropped screenshot if it demonstrates a broken image or wrong option. This makes the request easier to investigate than a general complaint about all photos. Keep the original reply with the record so that later follow-up refers to the same unresolved point.

For example, a hypothetical request could say that the front image is visible but the size label is unreadable, and that the saved order option is medium. The useful question is whether a readable label image can be provided before the applicable decision deadline. This is a sample evidence structure, not a promise that an extra image is free or ready within a fixed time. Confirm the actual service terms before proceeding.

## Approve the item only after resolving the gap

When new evidence appears, match it to the same order and compare it with the original request. A new general image does not answer a missing measurement. Record one clear outcome: keep the item, request clarification, or pursue the available return process. If several items are waiting for consolidation, keep the unresolved one identifiable instead of treating the entire batch as approved because most pictures look acceptable.

Finish by saving the accepted photo set and selected option with the purchase record. Then move to [parcel planning](/en/articles/hipobuy-shipping-calculator/), where packed weight, dimensions and route eligibility determine a separate decision. QC approval does not establish the final shipping charge. Keeping discovery, inspection and shipping records separate helps you resolve a future problem without confusing a public product image with evidence of the item actually received.
