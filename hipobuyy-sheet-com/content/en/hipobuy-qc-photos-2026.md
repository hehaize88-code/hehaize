## QC photos are evidence, not decoration

Hipobuy's public site says products purchased through the service are sent to its warehouse in China, where acceptance and quality inspection are performed and results are provided. That warehouse stage is useful because it gives you a chance to compare the received item with the seller listing before international shipping.

QC photos are not a guarantee of authenticity, durability or perfect quality. They are visual evidence. Use them to answer visible questions and to identify what is still unknown.

## Start by matching the order

Before judging quality, confirm that the images belong to the correct order and variant. Compare product type, color, size, quantity and included accessories with the saved listing. Keep the source URL and selected option beside the images.

A beautifully photographed wrong size is still the wrong item. Identity checks come before cosmetic checks.

## Review the whole item first

Look at overall shape and proportion before zooming into small details. For shoes, compare both sides, toe shape, heels, soles and visible labels. For hoodies and T-shirts, check front, back, sleeves, cuffs, hem and size label. For bags, inspect structure, handles, closures, bottom and interior where shown.

The necessary angles depend on the product. One standard photo set cannot answer every question.

## Check visible construction

Once identity is confirmed, inspect seams, stitching, printed details, hardware, stains, scuffs, dents, loose threads and missing components. Rank issues by whether they change your decision.

A tiny loose thread may not matter. A broken zipper, wrong color or missing accessory may matter immediately. Avoid treating every visible imperfection as equal.

## Compare printed and graphic items carefully

For jerseys, graphic tees and printed hoodies, compare the exact design option. Check spelling, number, patch, colorway and obvious placement. If the listing offered several designs under one product page, make sure you saved the selected option before purchase.

Do not use a logo in a QC photo as proof of authenticity. A photograph can show that a mark is present; it cannot establish the legal status or origin of the item.

## Use measurements where fit matters

A label can confirm marked size but cannot prove fit. For clothing, request the specific garment measurement you need when that option is available. For footwear, a clear label and relevant length measurement can reduce uncertainty.

Ask for one decision-relevant measurement rather than vague "better QC."

## Request targeted extra photos

A good request describes the missing evidence. Examples:

- "Show both heel areas straight on."
- "Photograph the inside size label clearly."
- "Show the zipper and surrounding stitching."
- "Photograph the stain under direct light."
- "Show the bottom and both corners of the bag."

Targeted requests are easier to evaluate when the reply arrives.

## Understand lighting and camera limitations

Warehouse lighting can make color appear warmer, cooler or brighter than the seller image. Wide-angle phone cameras can distort shape near the frame edge. Glare can resemble scratches, while low resolution can hide fine details.

If a decision depends on color or surface condition, ask for another angle or neutral-light image rather than drawing a strong conclusion from one ambiguous photo.

## Separate QC from seller claims

A seller may describe material, technology or performance in the listing. QC images generally cannot verify those claims. The same is true for long-term durability. Keep visible inspection separate from claims that require documentation or testing.

Hipobuy product pages currently include a disclaimer that third-party product quality and authenticity are not guaranteed. That makes evidence discipline especially important.

## Make a clear decision

Finish QC with one of four statuses: approve, request more evidence, exchange or return. Do not leave a known issue unresolved while submitting the parcel.

If return timing matters, check the current policy immediately. Storage periods and return windows are separate concepts.

## Record the evidence chain

Save the original product URL, selected variant, QC image set, extra-photo request if any, support response and final decision. If a later issue occurs, this record helps distinguish what was visible before shipping from what happened during transport or after delivery.

For a spreadsheet site, link QC guidance from product categories and article pages. That gives users a coherent path from product discovery to warehouse approval.

## QC by product type

Shoes: pair consistency, size labels, visible damage, soles, box decision.

Clothing: selected color, size label, prints, closures, seams, measurements.

Bags: dimensions, handles, closures, corners, included straps and hardware.

Accessories: quantity, selected color, visible components and packaging.

The checklist changes, but the principle stays the same: match first, inspect second, decide third.
