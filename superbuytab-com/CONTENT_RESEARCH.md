### Conflicts handled

- Fee-page example uses a detailed-photo price that differs from its own service list. No fixed detailed-photo price is published in our content.
- A shipping example labels a total 428 while displayed components 320+50+38 total 408. That arithmetic is not reproduced. Our arithmetic examples are explicitly illustrative and not quotations.
- English labels for purchasing expert assistance and shipping assistance are inconsistent across pages. Article tells readers to verify the actual scope rather than equating those services.
- 1688 policy has distinct seller classes. No universal number of photos or universal return entitlement is claimed.
- Older app/forwarding guides contain wording that can conflict about professional inspection. We use the narrower, evidenced description and explicitly explain photographic limitations.
