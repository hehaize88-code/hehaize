Superbuy shipping to the USA does not have one reliable price per kilogram. The useful quote depends on your destination, warehouse, item categories, packed weight, outer dimensions and the routes available when you submit the parcel. A price shared by another buyer may describe a different package and different service conditions.

This guide explains how to build a comparison for your own shipment. Start with the [official shipping calculator](https://www.superbuy.com/en/page/query/freight/) and keep your assumptions alongside the result. Treat a quote as a dated planning record. It is not a guarantee of future availability, delivery time or the complete amount payable at destination.

## Prepare a parcel record before comparing prices

List the items you intend to ship and any characteristics relevant to transport eligibility. Use the real product category and disclose features such as batteries or liquids where applicable. A cheaper result obtained by entering an inaccurate category is not a usable alternative for the parcel you actually have.

Record the destination accurately, including the address details requested during submission. Do not assume that every address within the USA has identical service availability or surcharges. Check the selected route's destination conditions and ask for clarification if your address has a characteristic the route treats differently.

Separate confirmed information from estimates. Before warehouse arrival, product weight and dimensions may be incomplete. After packing, use the outer parcel measurements rather than the dimensions of the largest item inside it. Keep the date and source of each measurement so that later comparisons remain understandable.

## Use consistent inputs in the shipping calculator

The official calculator asks for the destination, warehouse, weight, dimensions and item category. Enter the units shown in the form. A kilogram value entered into a grams field will describe a different parcel, and an item measurement cannot stand in for the final carton without an explicit assumption.

Run a baseline calculation using the most realistic information available. Save the inputs, route name, displayed price and date together. Then compare other eligible routes using that same parcel. If you change the dimensions while changing the route, you no longer have a clean comparison of the services alone.

Our [shipping calculator guide](/articles/superbuy-shipping-calculator/) explains actual and volumetric weight. The route's own billing rules matter, including its divisor, rounding and limits. Do not apply a divisor taken from a general example to every route without checking the selected service.

## Compare routes using more than the headline charge

Build a short comparison of the services that accept your actual parcel. Read the current route description before deciding whether a cheaper price meets your needs. A service that excludes your item category or cannot serve the destination is not a practical budget option.

| Comparison field | What to record | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Accepted contents and destination | The parcel must qualify for the service |
| Billing | Weight rule, rounding and size limits | Similar physical weights can produce different charges |
| Charges | Included services and possible additions | The displayed amount may not describe every cost |
| Delivery estimate | Stated range and its conditions | An estimate is not an appointment |
| Tracking | Available tracking and delivery information | Updates may differ between stages |
| Support terms | Coverage, exclusions and claim process | Problems need the correct evidence and deadline |

Keep screenshots or notes of the terms relevant to your decision. If the service description changes before submission, review the current version. An earlier screenshot explains what you considered, but it does not guarantee that an expired option remains available.

## Separate shipping charges from import charges

Keep the transport quote separate from any destination taxes, duties, brokerage or handling unless the current route clearly explains what is included. A route name or a phrase used in a forum is not enough to establish the treatment of your shipment. Ask the provider to explain unclear charge lines before you pay.

Do not rely on an old low-value exemption claim or assume that a familiar threshold settles the question. Import treatment can depend on the goods, origin, value, entry method and rules in force when the shipment arrives. This article deliberately does not publish a universal duty-free allowance or a fixed tax percentage for all USA parcels.

Use the [US Customs and Border Protection import information](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/basic-import-export) as the starting point for current official guidance. Check the requirements that apply to your actual goods and shipment. Keep purchase records and provide accurate descriptions and values through the required process; a shipping estimate is not a customs determination.

## Compare packing options without weakening protection

Ask which packaging must remain and which parts can be changed. A retail shoe box, a fragile object and folded clothing have different needs. Reducing empty space can affect a quote, but the relevant comparison is between suitable packing plans, not between a protected parcel and an unrealistic unprotected one.

Write down whether boxes may be removed, folded or must stay intact. Resolve return or exchange questions first. If an item is still under inspection, keep the packaging needed for that process and avoid asking the warehouse to make an irreversible change before the issue is settled.

Use our [packing guide](/articles/superbuy-packing/) to prepare a clear request. Recalculate using the confirmed outer dimensions after any agreed change. If the revised measurements alter route eligibility, compare the services that now apply rather than keeping an earlier route simply because it was your first choice.

## Evaluate combining or splitting parcels carefully

Combining items can simplify administration, but it is not automatically cheaper or more suitable. A larger parcel may cross a weight band, become limited by dimensions or include an item category that changes the eligible services. Compare the complete combined parcel against the complete alternatives.

For a split, record each parcel's contents, weight, dimensions and total displayed charges. Include any repeated packaging or service costs in the comparison. Do not compare the combined quote with only the cheaper half of a split shipment. The relevant number is the total for all parcels that would actually be sent.

Choose grouping for legitimate handling, service and budget reasons. Splitting does not remove the need for accurate declarations or compliance with the destination's rules. If the appropriate treatment is unclear, ask the provider or the relevant authority about the actual shipment rather than relying on a generic social-media rule.

## Set expectations for delivery and tracking

Use the selected route's current delivery estimate as a planning range. Do not treat it as a guaranteed arrival date for a trip, gift or event unless the service explicitly offers an applicable guarantee. Leave room for the stages that occur before and after the main international transport leg.

Save the parcel reference and tracking number when they become available. Tracking messages describe events recorded by the logistics system; they do not always reveal the exact physical position of a parcel at every moment. Read our [tracking guide](/articles/superbuy-tracking/) before interpreting a quiet period as a confirmed loss.

If you need support, provide the parcel reference, latest visible event and the question you need answered. Follow the current service's enquiry and claim procedure. Avoid submitting conflicting requests through several channels without preserving the same facts and references.

## Check the complete budget before submission

Review the item costs, domestic delivery, selected services, international transport and any destination charges you need to budget for. Our [fees guide](/articles/superbuy-fees/) separates those stages. Keep estimated amounts labelled as estimates until the relevant charges are confirmed.

Before submitting, confirm the address, contents, route, packing instructions and unresolved inspection questions. Save the final parcel details and payment record. If the shipping amount is later reconciled, compare the adjustment with the final measurements and relevant bill instead of an unrelated early estimate.

For shoes or clothing, start from the [shoes collection](/categories/shoes/) or [hoodies collection](/categories/hoodies-sweaters/) and make the buying and shipping decisions together. Understanding the likely parcel before ordering gives you a more realistic budget than choosing products first and considering transport only after everything reaches the warehouse.
