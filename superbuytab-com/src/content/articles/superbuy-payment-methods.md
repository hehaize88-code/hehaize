Superbuy's current official pages mention Wise, international credit cards, WeChat Pay, Alipay and Chinese banking options. This is evidence of published payment guidance, not a guarantee that every method appears for every account, country or transaction. Use the options actually displayed in the current payment flow before committing money.

The most important distinction is between adding funds to an account and settling a particular order or parcel charge. A successful transfer to the platform does not necessarily show that the intended purchase has been paid. Check the transaction record and the relevant order separately, especially when one still appears pending.

## Identify what you are paying for

Start by naming the charge: goods, domestic delivery, optional service or international parcel. The official shopping workflow separates item payment from the later shipping payment. If you only record a single unexplained amount, it becomes difficult to tell whether a later request is a new stage, an adjustment or a duplicate.

Keep the order or parcel reference beside the payment reference. Record the displayed currency, amount due and any fees shown before confirmation. For a top-up, record both the amount you send and the amount expected to reach the account. Use the [fees guide](/articles/superbuy-fees/) to connect these transactions to the overall purchase budget.

Review any outstanding message before paying. A seller price change, an unavailable option or a packing request may need a decision first. Payment should follow the agreed specification, not substitute for resolving it. Confirm that the item or shipping choice shown on the payment screen is the one you intend to authorise.

## Compare the methods offered in your account

Choose among the methods currently available for the actual transaction. Compare the amount credited, the displayed processing charge, currency conversion, confirmation process and any relevant refund conditions. A familiar provider name does not establish that the final cost or processing time matches another merchant's payment flow.

| Payment route | What to check before authorising | What to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International card | Displayed amount, currency and verification request | Transaction reference and final status |
| Wise or bank transfer | Current recipient instructions and required reference | Transfer receipt and account credit record |
| Supported wallet | Availability in the current flow and shown fees | Provider confirmation and order record |
| Account balance | Available funds and the specific charge selected | Balance movement and settled order reference |

The table is a comparison checklist, not an assertion that every option is currently enabled for you. If a method is absent, ask official support about the available alternatives. Avoid following old instructions that name a recipient or payment route different from the one shown by the current official process.

## Pay attention to card verification

For a card payment, review the billing details and any verification step provided by the bank or payment processor. If verification is interrupted, first check the final transaction status. A failed browser return, a temporary bank authorization and a completed platform payment are different observations that need to be reconciled.

If the bank declines the transaction, ask the bank about the actual decline and check whether the platform offers another supported method. Repeatedly submitting the same charge without understanding the result can make the payment history harder to interpret. It also creates more records to distinguish when asking support for help.

Use only the official payment flow and the bank's own verification channel. Do not share passwords, verification codes or full card details with someone offering to complete a payment privately. A support request should identify the transaction and the problem while keeping sensitive authentication information out of ordinary messages.

## Follow the current Wise or transfer instructions

When a transfer option is offered, read the recipient details, currency, reference and amount instructions shown for that operation. Do not reuse a screenshot from an old tutorial as proof that the same transfer details still apply. Check whether the displayed amount describes money sent or the amount expected to be credited after charges.

Keep the receipt and any transfer reference until the balance or order shows the expected credit. A provider marking a transfer complete and the platform matching it to your account can be separate events. If the platform requests evidence, provide the relevant receipt through its official support process.

Do not choose a transfer based only on a headline fee. Compare the final amount credited on the same currency basis as the other methods you are considering. An exchange-rate difference can affect the result even when a separate processing fee looks smaller. The useful comparison is the complete transaction, not one isolated line.

## Separate a top-up from an order payment

After adding funds, review the balance details and then open the intended order or parcel. Confirm whether payment has actually been applied to that record. If the order remains unpaid, do not assume that the money disappeared; first check whether it is available as account balance or awaiting confirmation.

The official mobile account includes balance details and recharge confirmation information. Use those records to trace the movement of funds. If the transfer is visible but the intended charge is not settled, ask support which step remains. The [order status guide](/articles/superbuy-order-status/) explains why payment and purchasing labels should be read together.

For personal budgeting, adding money and later spending that same balance are not two independent purchases. Record the funding movement separately from the goods or shipping expense. Otherwise, adding both to the final cost can double-count the same money and make the order seem more expensive than it was.

## Investigate failed or pending payments before repeating them

Compare the platform status with the payment provider's final record. Save the amount, currency, time and transaction reference. Identify whether the provider shows a completed payment, a pending authorization, a reversal or a decline. Avoid replacing these details with a cropped screenshot that only shows the account balance.

If the provider shows payment but the platform does not, ask official support to match the transaction. If the provider shows only a pending authorization, ask the provider what it means for that transaction. There is no single waiting period that can safely be applied to every bank, payment method and failure condition.

Before retrying, establish whether the original attempt could still complete. If support recommends another payment, retain that instruction and the original reference. Afterwards, reconcile both records. This prevents a troubleshooting step from becoming an unexplained second charge in your budget.

## Can you pay with PayPal or use a coupon?

Do not rely on an old screenshot or an outdated guide to establish PayPal availability. Current payment guidance and historical pages can differ. Check whether the method is actually offered for your transaction and ask official support when necessary. This guide does not claim that PayPal is universally available or universally unavailable.

A coupon is also conditional. Confirm which charge it applies to, whether your order qualifies and whether the discount is accepted before payment. Keep the undiscounted amount visible until the payment screen shows the reduction. A shipping promotion does not automatically reduce the earlier purchase cost or a payment provider's own charge.

If you are comparing payment methods because the total looks high, first identify the source of the increase. The [shipping cost checklist](/articles/why-superbuy-shipping-expensive/) helps separate parcel pricing from payment costs. Switching payment methods will not by itself correct an oversized parcel estimate or an incorrectly selected shipping service.

## Track refunds and final adjustments separately

Read the actual refund decision and destination of the funds. An order cancellation, a balance credit and a withdrawal to a payment provider are different events. Keep each reference until the result is visible in the relevant account. Do not promise yourself a fixed refund arrival date from another customer's experience.

International shipping can also involve an estimate followed by settlement. Compare any adjustment with the original deposit and the detailed explanation. The finished budget should show each real expense once, with refunds and corrections clearly identified. That record is more useful for your next order than a generic claim that one payment method is always cheapest.
