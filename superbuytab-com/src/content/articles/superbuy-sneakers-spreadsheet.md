A Superbuy sneakers spreadsheet helps you discover shoe listings, but a photograph and a size label cannot tell you everything about a pair. The useful next step is to connect the listing to a specific seller option, a measurement you understand and a warehouse inspection plan. That sequence reduces avoidable uncertainty before international shipping makes a correction more difficult.

Start with our [shoes collection](/categories/shoes/) for current listings, then use this guide to compare the pairs you actually intend to buy. SuperbuyTab is an independent discovery site. A listing is not an authenticity certificate, a stock guarantee or a promise that the pair will fit. Product photos, prices and available options need to be checked on the current order page.

## Build a small shortlist with useful details

Open the [shoe spreadsheet](/spreadsheet/?category=shoes) and shortlist a few pairs that match your intended use. Record the original listing, selected colour, seller size, displayed item price and any domestic delivery charge. If a listing contains several designs, keep the exact option name with the link. The same page can contain variants with different prices and availability.

Avoid comparing only the cheapest displayed amount. It may belong to a different option from the one pictured in your notes. Reopen the selected variant before ordering and check whether the seller provides measurements, materials or a clear description of the contents. If an important detail is missing, ask before paying rather than treating the photo as an answer.

Keep your shortlist manageable. Comparing a few complete records is more useful than saving many links without knowing which size or colour you meant. Remove unavailable options and keep a fallback pair only if you would genuinely be happy to receive it.

## Compare measurements instead of converting labels blindly

Shoe labels are not a dependable substitute for measurements. Different sellers can use different charts, and a familiar regional size does not establish the internal space of an unfamiliar shoe. Compare the seller's chart with a measurement method you can reproduce, and confirm whether the chart describes foot length, insole length or another dimension.

Measure both feet using the same method and consider the socks you expect to wear. If one foot is larger, use that information when asking about fit. Do not add an arbitrary allowance to a chart without understanding what its numbers represent. A foot-length recommendation and a removable-insole measurement answer different questions.

An existing comfortable pair can provide a reference, but its label alone is weak evidence. Record the model, the relevant measurements and what feels comfortable about it. For a broader method, read our [sizing guide](/articles/superbuy-sizing/) before you select the seller's option.

## Ask a question that the seller can answer

If you are between sizes, describe the uncertainty precisely. Ask which measurement the chart uses, whether the selected option follows that chart and whether the shoe has any fit information the seller can confirm. A vague request for the best size leaves too much room for assumptions about comfort and intended use.

Do not assume that an agent can guarantee fit from a photograph. If a measurement service is available, request the particular dimension you need and ask how it will be measured. Some shoe constructions make a useful internal measurement difficult. A refusal or limitation is information to consider before proceeding, not evidence that the pair must fit anyway.

Keep the seller's response with the order reference. If the delivered option differs from the agreed option, that record can make the issue easier to explain. It still does not replace the seller's return conditions or the inspection deadline shown for your order.

## Check warehouse photos against your order

When the pair reaches the warehouse, compare the photos with the order you placed. Start with the selected colour, model details and size label. Then inspect both shoes together where the available angles allow it. Look for visible differences between the left and right shoe, damage, missing components and anything that appears inconsistent with the listing.

| Check | What to compare | When to ask for more evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected option | Order record and visible labels | The label is hidden or unclear |
| Pair consistency | Both shoes in comparable views | Only one shoe is visible |
| Visible condition | Upper, sole and obvious seams | A mark could be lighting or damage |
| Measurements | Agreed method and reference points | The ruler position is ambiguous |
| Included items | Listing contents and received contents | An expected component is missing |

Warehouse photos are a practical visual check, not a laboratory test or an authenticity assessment. They cannot establish long-term durability, comfort or every hidden defect. Our [QC photo guide](/articles/superbuy-qc-photos/) explains how to request an additional angle that answers a specific question.

## Resolve problems before changing the packaging

If the size label or visible condition raises a concern, pause the parcel submission and contact support with the order reference. Explain the difference between what you ordered and what the photos show. Include the relevant photo and ask which resolution options remain available under the current seller and platform conditions.

Keep the original packaging while a return or exchange is being considered. Authorising box removal too early can complicate the next step, especially if packaging is relevant to the seller's conditions. Do not assume that international return shipping will be economical after the parcel has left the warehouse.

Read the [returns guide](/articles/superbuy-returns/) and the live order terms before deciding to keep, exchange or return the pair. A clear inspection decision should come before a packing decision. Shipping an unresolved issue simply moves the same uncertainty farther away.

## Decide whether the shoe box is worth keeping

Keeping a retail box can matter for storage, protection or your own preference. Removing it can change the parcel dimensions, but it does not automatically make every route cheaper. The result depends on the final packaging, the route's billing rule and whether the shoes still need substantial protection.

Ask for a comparison based on the actual packed parcel. State whether the retail box must remain intact, may be folded or may be discarded. Different instructions lead to different outcomes. If the box matters, make that priority explicit instead of asking only for the smallest possible parcel.

Consider the construction of the shoes. Structured uppers and delicate details may need protection against crushing or rubbing. The [packing guide](/articles/superbuy-packing/) helps you write a brief that balances protection and dimensions without relying on a blanket instruction for every item.

## Compare the complete cost of your shortlist

Your budget should include the selected item, domestic delivery where applicable, any chosen services and international shipping. Separate destination charges from the shipping quote unless the route explicitly explains what it includes. A low item price can still produce an unattractive total when the parcel is bulky or requires extra handling.

Use the [shipping calculator guide](/articles/superbuy-shipping-calculator/) to compare complete parcel assumptions. Keep the destination and contents consistent when comparing routes. If you compare keeping and removing a box, record both sets of outer measurements and the corresponding protection plan.

Do not divide a total parcel bill equally among items and then mistake that allocation for a quoted price per pair. It is a personal budgeting method. Adding or removing one pair can change dimensions, weight bands and route eligibility, so the next parcel may behave differently.

## Finish with a documented buying decision

Before paying for shipping, check that the selected pair, size evidence, inspection decision and packing instructions agree. Save the order and parcel references so that a later question can be traced to the correct item. If you changed your mind about keeping the box, update the instruction before the packing work is performed.

Return to the [shoes collection](/categories/shoes/) when you are ready to compare current listings. Use the shortlist to make a decision based on the available evidence, rather than treating the number of saved links as progress. A pair that meets your requirements with clear information is a better candidate than a cheaper pair whose important details remain unknown.
