A Superbuy order status describes a stage of the purchasing or warehouse process. It is not always an international tracking update. First identify whether you are looking at an item order, a payment record or a submitted parcel. Then read the detailed event and any request for action attached to that record.

The current mobile account separates orders from parcels. Labels include Unpaid, Pending, Signed and In Storage for orders, and Unpaid, Unshipped, Shipped and Back Payment for parcels. Wording can vary between interfaces. This guide explains the practical checks behind those stages without promising a universal processing deadline.

## Start with the correct record

Keep the original product link, selected option, quantity and order reference together. If you have already submitted a parcel, add its separate reference. A screenshot showing only a status word leaves out the information needed to tell which item or shipment is affected. Save the timestamp and the most recent message as well.

Superbuy's buying guide separates paying for the item, agent purchasing, warehouse inspection and international delivery. That sequence explains why a paid order can still be waiting for the seller, and why an item in storage may not yet have an international tracking number. Read the [buying guide](/articles/superbuy-spreadsheet-guide/) if the overall process is unfamiliar.

When contacting support, describe the unresolved stage instead of saying only that the parcel is late. For example, explain that payment was recorded but an item still needs a seller update. This gives the team a specific question to investigate and avoids confusing domestic delivery with the later international shipment.

## What should you check when an order is unpaid or pending?

For an unpaid record, inspect the payment history and the amount still due before trying again. A bank authorization or transfer receipt does not by itself prove that the platform has credited the order. If your money appears to have left the payment account, retain the reference and ask for reconciliation rather than immediately creating duplicate payments.

Pending is a broad label, so open the order details. Look for a missing option, a price adjustment, a purchasing question or another message that needs your answer. The label alone cannot tell you whether the agent is waiting for you or carrying out a normal step. Use the latest detailed message to decide what action is appropriate.

The [payment methods guide](/articles/superbuy-payment-methods/) explains the difference between adding account funds and paying a particular charge. Check both records when necessary. Do not send payment credentials or full card details in an ordinary support message; the transaction reference and the affected order normally provide a safer starting point.

## Purchased does not mean dispatched internationally

If an order detail shows that the item was purchased, the seller stage and domestic movement may still be outstanding. Check whether a seller dispatch update or domestic tracking reference exists. Your international parcel normally requires later warehouse processing and a separate submission, so an absent international tracking number at this stage is not surprising.

Superbuy's help centre describes an order enquiry function and a delivery reminder option for eligible shopping orders. Use the action offered on your actual order and read its conditions. A reminder can request information from the seller; it cannot guarantee stock availability or force a carrier to deliver at a particular time.

If the seller cannot supply the selected variant, ask what alternatives or cancellation options apply before approving a replacement. Keep the original specification visible. A different size or bundle can affect the price, inspection needs and eventual parcel, so resolving the change is more useful than simply trying to move the status forward.

## Signed and in storage are different checkpoints

A domestic delivery event indicating receipt at the warehouse is not the same as the item becoming available for parcel submission. Intake, identification and the applicable inspection work can occur after receipt. Check the order detail for the actual warehouse update and any request to resolve a mismatch.

When the item becomes available in storage, review the evidence for your own order. Compare quantity, selected variant, visible labels and condition with the original specification. The [QC photos guide](/articles/superbuy-qc-photos/) helps you request a useful additional view when an important detail is missing. Catalogue images are not inspection evidence for your individual purchase.

If something is wrong, raise it while the relevant return or after-sales process remains available. Storage time and return eligibility are separate issues. Read the [returns guide](/articles/superbuy-returns/) before removing packaging or authorising international delivery of an item with an unresolved problem.

## Match the next action to the stage

| Record or stage | First check | Useful next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unpaid order | Payment history and amount due | Resolve an uncredited transaction before repeating payment |
| Pending order | Detailed message or missing choice | Answer the specific purchasing question |
| Purchased item | Seller dispatch and domestic updates | Request a seller update when appropriate |
| Warehouse receipt | Intake and inspection information | Confirm whether the item is available in storage |
| Stored item | Your inspection evidence | Resolve problems before parcel submission |
| Submitted parcel | Payment and packing details | Review the shipment record separately |

This table is a practical interpretation of the workflow, not a replacement for the exact explanation shown in your account. If a label conflicts with a detailed message, ask support to identify the current stage. Avoid making an irreversible packing decision from a status word alone.

## Understand unpaid, unshipped and shipped parcels

After selecting stored items and submitting delivery details, inspect the parcel record. An unpaid parcel needs its own payment review even if all item purchases were settled earlier. Compare the selected service, destination and packing request before approving the shipping charge. The [shipping calculator guide](/articles/superbuy-shipping-calculator/) explains the inputs behind an estimate.

For an unshipped parcel, look for outstanding payment, packing work or an instruction requiring confirmation. Do not assume the label proves a carrier delay when the parcel has not yet been handed over. Ask which preparation step remains open and whether you need to respond.

Once the parcel is shipped, move to the carrier event history. A shipping label or tracking reference may appear before a later physical scan. The [tracking guide](/articles/superbuy-tracking/) focuses on the international journey, including gaps between events. Keep that investigation separate from the status of the original purchased items.

## Review a back payment or shipping adjustment

The mobile parcel area includes a Back Payment label. If it appears for your shipment, open the associated record and read the stated reason and amount. Do not infer from the label alone that the same purchase has been charged twice. Compare it with the original shipping deposit and any later settlement information.

The official fee explanation describes estimated international shipping followed by reconciliation with the logistics provider's bill. This is a reason to keep the initial charge and later adjustment distinct in your budget. It does not establish the cause of every individual payment request; use the itemised explanation in your account.

If the reason is unclear, ask for the calculation or service detail before paying. The [fees guide](/articles/superbuy-fees/) explains how to record a final adjustment without adding the original deposit again. Retain the response and final transaction reference so the completed total can be checked later.

## Send one clear support request

Include the order or parcel reference, the relevant date, the latest status, the last detailed event and the question you need answered. Explain any action already taken, such as replying to an option request or submitting a payment reference. Avoid opening repeated conversations that omit the earlier answer.

Ask for a concrete next step: whether a seller update is available, whether intake is complete, whether a payment needs matching or which packing task is still outstanding. Do not invent a deadline from another customer's experience. Processing time depends on the stage and circumstances of your own order.
