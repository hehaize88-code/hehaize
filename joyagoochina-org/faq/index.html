Before you use a Joyagoo spreadsheet

A spreadsheet helps you discover items. Your decision still depends on the live listing, seller terms, QC photos and available shipping line.

Where do the product links go?

Each product card opens its matching product detail page. Category cards open the matching catalogue section; no product button leads to an unrelated website.

Does Joyagoo inspect products at the warehouse?

Published shopping guidance says warehouse staff perform a quality check and provide photos after the item arrives.

How long is the return request window?

The published policy describes a five-day or 120-hour request window after QC completion, subject to seller eligibility and product conditions.

Is this website operated by Joyagoo?

No. This is an independent discovery and educational website. It does not process payments, orders, QC or international parcels.

Is the Joyagoo shopping-agent service free?

The published fee explanation describes the shopping-agent service as free. You still pay for the product, Chinese domestic shipping, international shipping and any optional or return-related services you choose.

Why are there two main payments?

The first payment covers the product and domestic delivery to the warehouse. International shipping is selected and paid after the stored items are submitted as a parcel.

How long is free warehouse storage?

The published shopping guide states 90 days of free storage for each product. This is separate from the much shorter five-day return-request window after QC.

How much does an extra QC photo cost?

The published value-added-services table lists a detailed photo at $0.15 each, whether requested during ordering or after warehouse arrival.

How much is rehearsal packing?

Two official pages currently differ: the shopping guide says 20 CNY, while the value-added-services table says $3 per parcel. Check the live account price before ordering.

Can I request parcel photos?

The published service table lists parcel photography at $0.75 per parcel and describes one photo before sealing and one after sealing.

How long can international delivery take?

Published general ranges are 10–20 days by air, 45–85 days by ocean and 40–60 days by rail after warehouse departure. Destination, route and customs can change the result.

Can a coupon reduce the product price?

The published buying workflow applies coupons to international shipping. It says coupons cannot be stacked and does not apply them to the seller’s product price.

Do customer reviews guarantee my result?

No. Reviews describe individual experiences. Seller rules, product eligibility, destination, route and current platform information still determine the outcome.
