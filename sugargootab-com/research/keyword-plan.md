# Sugargoo keyword research and first ten article briefs

Research date: 2026-10-18 (Asia/Shanghai). Scope: the new Sugargoo preview using cnbuycha product data. This is a qualitative public-search and competitor-topic study, not a keyword-volume report. No Search Console, Ads Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, click estimates, rankings or difficulty scores were supplied. Priority below means usefulness to this site's shopping journey, not measured demand.

## Findings and editorial boundaries

- Public results repeatedly connect the head term `sugargoo spreadsheet` with finds, QC photos, first-order guides, shipping estimation and direct marketplace links. Navigation should connect the spreadsheet, FAQ and articles rather than publish ten disconnected keyword pages.
- The homepage should remain the main commercial landing page for `sugargoo spreadsheet`. Article 1 targets the narrower **how-to** intent and should not compete with the homepage title.
- A competitor, sugargoowithqc.com, puts product discovery, QC and link conversion together. Its homepage heading is “Sugargoo spreadsheet with QC photos.” The source, counts and verification claims on that site belong to that site and must not be transferred to this project.
- sugargoo.shop/guides/ groups its published guide headings around searching, warehouse checks, parcels, shipping, tracking and after-sales. This confirms a broad informational cluster; it does not establish traffic, accuracy, or search volume. Its body content is not used as editorial source material.
- Other results include sugargoolinks.com/guide (beginner ordering), sugargoo-spreadsheets.com (fees, storage and returns), and JadeShip's link converter. Inspecting their topic labels helps distinguish intents. No rankings, article text, statistics or unsupported claims should be copied.
- Our useful difference: show the buyer exactly what to verify and at which point, link to an official source, and distinguish catalogue pictures from the buyer's own warehouse evidence. The preview currently has reference product images, not verified personal warehouse QC. Image search is not connected. Do not imply either feature is available.
- Keep articles as editorial prose and decision checklists. The user's requested 20+ FAQ belongs to `/faq/`, not repeated as padding in all ten articles. Use no fabricated first-person purchases, test parcels, savings, delivery outcomes, ratings or product rankings.
- Official policy can change. Dates and exact figures must be tied to a current official source and the relevant order type. “Check your account” is a useful final verification step, not a substitute for explaining the underlying process.

## Ten article briefs

The proposed titles are original editorial titles. Each article should have a stable slug, a clear introduction, substantive sections, one useful table or worked example when appropriate, an official source list and contextual links to two related articles. Aim for useful depth rather than a padded word count. If the project retains a 1,200–1,800 English-word standard, write to that range with distinct practical content. Never auto-publish untranslated English under another HTML language.

### 1. How to Use a Sugargoo Spreadsheet: From Search to a Shortlist

- Primary keyword: `how to use sugargoo spreadsheet`.
- Support phrases: `sugargoo finds`, `sugargoo spreadsheet search`, `sugargoo product list`, `sugargoo spreadsheet links`.
- Intent/audience: discovery guidance for a first-time catalogue visitor; not a generic platform introduction.
- Priority: first; establishes the bridge from homepage/catalogue to the editorial hub.
- Slug: `how-to-use-sugargoo-spreadsheet`.
- Original outline: define what a recommendation directory does; start with use/size/budget needs; search broad terms then refine category; compare the same variation rather than lowest displayed price; record two or three candidates and missing facts; open source/current checkout; decide which candidate merits an order. Include a comparison worksheet with blank fields, not fabricated goods.
- Useful difference: explain actual live site controls, category filtering and the meaning of catalogue price. Only describe filters or sorting the preview genuinely provides. The image-search state must be truthful.
- Evidence: competitor topic E1/E2 above; official ordering O1/O2 for the handoff. Site source code is authoritative for local controls.
- Avoid overlap: no full payment, QC or shipping tutorial; link to those articles.
- Gaps: no evidence of user search volume; current catalogue images are reference photos and must not be described as QC-verified.
