# Sugargoo official fact ledger for preview FAQ and guides

Research date: 2026-10-18. Internal editorial research, not customer-facing copy.

## Method and limits

- Sources below are exclusively official `blog.sugargoo.com` pages; no anonymous forum claims are treated as policy.
- Engine 1 returned mostly unrelated results on two attempts. Engine 2 retrieved indexed official page content. Direct web-open requests to four blog articles returned HTTP 403; this research does not claim a successful live full-page fetch for those URLs. The search index usually reports recent crawling, but the policies themselves often date to 2025 or early 2026.
- Official blog descriptions are guidance, not proof of every checkout/account entitlement. Prefer current account screens, selected route terms, and support for time-sensitive details.
- Do not reproduce articles. Use short factual paraphrases, original analysis, practical checklists and clearly hypothetical examples. Each retrieved source carried a 200-word derived-content limit. Multiple translations of the same facts and ten articles should not simply reproduce the same source at length.

## Important editorial decisions

1. **Warehouse conflict:** current official blog home says 90 days; dedicated storage article (updated 2025-11-25) says purchasing 100 days and forwarding/resell 30 days. FAQ should explicitly acknowledge different published figures and direct readers to each item's Packing Center countdown. Do not make an unconditional 100-day promise.
2. **QC counts conflict:** ordinary agent-purchase articles consistently say five product QC images. Dedicated QC policy mentions one final parcel image, while an older clothing article describes seven total images as five product plus two package. Avoid promising a fixed package-photo count. Our cnbuycha catalog images are reference images, not demonstrated personal warehouse QC.
3. **QC scope:** ordinary QC is not authentication, a laboratory test, or electronic functionality testing. Sealed goods normally receive exterior inspection; safety screening may require opening. Different order types have different checks.
4. **Returns:** the official 2026-01-07 return guide describes a five-day warehouse window for eligible, unchanged goods where the seller supports the return. It is not an unconditional right for every product. Later requests require negotiation. Do not guarantee timing or a full refund.
5. **Insurance:** the detailed insurance guide, updated 2026-06-29, includes route exclusions and excludes product damage. An older/general article loosely mentions damage, so use the detailed policy and selected-route terms. Never promise all-risk insurance, automatic customs reimbursement, or guaranteed delivery.
6. **Fees/payment:** zero purchasing service fee claims concern standard Taobao orders; paid services, domestic freight, international freight, payment/FX costs may remain. Checkout methods vary. Do not copy older PayPal claims as universal availability.
7. **Customs:** omit country-specific tax thresholds and under-declaration tips from old marketing posts. Direct readers to destination customs rules, correct product information and actual route terms.
8. **Coupons:** no permanent CNY 800 claim, old promotional code, or blanket saving percentage. Explain checking minimum spend, expiry, eligible charges, route restrictions and stacking in the account.

## FAQ seeds (36, original concise factual answers)

These are research seeds. Preserve qualifiers in every language. Source IDs are explicit so implementation can expose a Sources link or local source list.

1. **What does Sugargoo do?** It purchases from Chinese sellers on a buyer's behalf, receives goods at its warehouse and offers international forwarding after the buyer submits a parcel. [S06]
2. **Does the first payment include delivery to my country?** Normally the product and Chinese domestic freight are paid first; international freight is paid when the warehouse parcel is ready. Optional services may add costs. [S07]
3. **Do I select size and color again?** Confirm the actual listing's options, quantity and remarks before payment. A recommendation card alone does not establish the ordered variant. [S24]
4. **Can I use a product link?** Sugargoo accepts supported product-page URLs. Use the exact item link, not a shop homepage or an incomplete sharing code. [S11]
5. **Why does a product link fail?** Delisted items, login-restricted pages, incomplete URLs and links to a shop rather than an item can prevent parsing. Confirm the original listing first. [S11]
6. **What is DIY Order?** It is manual submission when automatic parsing fails. Provide the item link, name, seller, variant notes, images if available, quantity, price and domestic freight information. Acceptance still depends on service restrictions. [S12]
7. **Are all purchasing services free?** The official Taobao guide describes free standard purchasing service. That does not remove product costs, freight, paid extras or payment-related charges. Check the full current total. [S10]
8. **Which payment methods can I use?** Official guidance describes international cards, wallets, regional methods and account balance. The choices and charges actually displayed for your account at checkout govern. [S08, S09]
9. **Why can my charged currency amount differ?** Checkout conversion and a payment provider's or bank's exchange rate or fees may affect the final charge. Review both before authorizing payment. [S08]
10. **When do warehouse QC photos appear?** They are part of processing after goods arrive and before overseas shipment. Check the order's warehouse status and uploaded evidence in the account. [S06]
11. **How many product QC pictures are included?** Official guidance describes five basic photos for ordinary purchasing orders. Forwarding and sealed goods have different inspection conditions; additional detail can require a paid service. [S03]
12. **Do QC photos prove authenticity or electronic function?** No. Official scope excludes guaranteed brand authentication and ordinary electronics functionality testing. Visible condition is a narrower check. [S04]
13. **Will factory-sealed packaging be opened?** Normally only its exterior is checked. Sugargoo reserves opening for suspected sensitive or prohibited contents; discuss any special requirement before purchase. [S04]
14. **Can I request a close-up or measurement?** Personalized photography and other detailed services may be available for a charge. Specify the feature you need and confirm the service scope. [S16]
15. **What if a QC image is unclear?** Contact official customer service with the order and the unclear image. Its QC guide says unclear free QC or parcel pictures can be retaken without charge. [S03]
16. **Is storage always 100 days?** Do not assume so: the official homepage and detailed storage article publish different figures, and order types differ. Follow the remaining-days indicator in your Packing Center. [S01, S02]
17. **When should I arrange a storage extension?** Check each item's countdown and arrange shipment or an available extension before expiry. The storage guide warns that expired unextended goods may be disposed of without compensation. [S02]
18. **How do I combine orders?** In Packing Center select eligible stored products and submit a parcel, then confirm address, services and a suitable route before paying international freight. [S15]
19. **Will consolidation always be cheaper?** It can share a route's initial charge, but package size, weight and route limits still determine the quote. Compare the final combined and split options. [S15; last sentence editorial advice]
20. **What does the shipping estimator need?** Destination and region, product category and weight in grams are core inputs. Include dimensions for bulky parcels and review route details. [S13]
21. **Is an estimated shipping cost the final bill?** No. Repacking, packaging materials and actual or volumetric weight can change the final cost. Use the completed parcel quote for payment decisions. [S14]
22. **What is volumetric weight?** It converts parcel dimensions into a billing weight using the route's divisor. The divisor is not universal; inspect the selected route's rule. [S14]
23. **What is rehearsal shipping?** It is a paid pre-shipment packing service used to obtain more accurate packed weight and dimensions. Start it from Packing Center and check its current fee before confirming. [S14]
24. **Which packing extras are useful?** Options include protective reinforcement and photography. Match an available service to the actual item and compare its added weight or cost; protection is not a guarantee against damage. [S16]
25. **Can I cancel an unpaid order?** The official cancellation guide permits canceling pending-payment orders in the account. Later stages require refund or after-sales handling and may involve the seller. [S17]
26. **Can I cancel after the seller has shipped?** Contact Sugargoo through the order or support. Cancellation then depends on seller coordination and the parcel's stage, rather than a guaranteed instant reversal. [S17]
27. **What is the return window?** The guide describes requests within five days of warehousing for eligible goods, with intact packaging and seller support. Apply promptly; a request is not automatic approval. [S18]
28. **Who pays domestic return postage?** Personal-reason returns normally leave the buyer paying return freight and unrecoverable costs. Seller-fault cases follow different allocation. Let Sugargoo confirm the approved calculation. [S18]
29. **Where does a refund arrive?** The return guide says approved refunds go to Sugargoo balance. Withdrawal to an available payment method is separate, and timing depends on processing and the provider. [S18]
30. **Are all products returnable?** No. Custom products and various opened, activated, second-hand or other excluded categories may not qualify. Verify seller and platform conditions before ordering. [S18]
31. **Does shipping insurance cover every problem?** No. The detailed policy has route-specific coverage and exclusions, including product damage. Read the policy for the selected route and parcel rather than assuming blanket protection. [S19]
32. **Can I add insurance after shipping?** The detailed guide says insurance cannot be added or its amount changed after dispatch. Decide before submitting the insured parcel. [S19]
33. **What evidence is needed for an insurance claim?** Requirements depend on the loss category. Preserve order values, parcel information and packaging evidence; official guidance gives a 72-hour reporting window for partial loss. Check current terms promptly. [S19]
34. **Can batteries or liquids use any route?** No. Sensitive goods need compatible handling and route approval, while prohibited goods cannot be accepted. Confirm product type, destination and route before buying. [S20]
35. **Why has tracking stopped updating?** Carrier handovers, customs processing, document requests or scan delays may produce gaps. Check the parcel's logistics record and ask support about unresolved or exceptional statuses. [S21, S22]
36. **Why does a coupon not apply?** Check expiry, minimum spend, eligible charges, new-user conditions and whether it has been used or can combine with another discount. The account's coupon terms take priority. [S23]
